# Code of conduct

Folio welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be considerate, give constructive feedback, and discuss ideas and code without attacking people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close conversations, or restrict participation in response to violations. Responses should be proportionate and prioritize the safety of participants.

To report a concern, use GitHub's reporting controls on the relevant content or contact a maintainer through a contact method they have made public. Do not repeat private or harmful material in a public issue.
